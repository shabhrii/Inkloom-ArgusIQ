# Future roadmap (Phase 3)

Only pursue after the core MVP and selected Phase (2) improvements are stable:
- Advanced policy compiler with human approval of extracted rules.
- Confidence calibration and abstention evaluation.
- Active learning based on reviewer disagreement.
- Expanded multimodal and video analysis.
- Advanced hybrid retrieval and reranking.
- Broader benchmark corpus and ablation studies.
- Configurable private inference and enterprise deployment.
- Advanced policy coverage and longitudinal analytics.

Every feature needs an acceptance test and must remain optional where possible.
